## 1. Ramanujan's 1/pi Series

来源：https://ramanujan.aimersociety.com/discoveries/ramanujan-one-over-pi-series

$$\frac{1}{\pi}=\frac{2\sqrt2}{9801}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(4n)!(1103+26390n)}{(n!)^4 396^{4n}}$$
